# SchoolBus Connect: 15-Minute School Manager Presentation & Demo Script

**Target Audience:** School Director, Principal, or Transportation Manager  
**Tone:** Plain language, short sentences, professional, no technical jargon  
**Duration:** ~15 minutes (Presentation: 10 mins, Discussion & Questions: 5 mins)  
**Data Safety Note:** All data shown during this presentation uses fictitious names, sample routes, and fake telephone numbers.

---

## 1. Opening (Problem & Solution)

Every morning and afternoon, parents call your front office or call drivers directly to ask one frantic question: *"Where is the bus right now?"* 

Drivers get distracted while driving, office phone lines get tied up during peak hours, and parents wait outside in bad weather when traffic causes unexpected delays.

**SchoolBus Connect** solves this problem by giving parents a simple screen on their phone showing where their child's bus is while it is on the road, while giving your school manager full control over routes, buses, and student safety.

  * Prominent transmission indicator: *'Location sent 3s ago (accuracy: 14 m)'*.

* **What to Click:** Tap the orange **Report Delay** button. Select **Heavy traffic** and tap **Submit Delay**.
* **Exactly What to Say:**

* **What the Audience Should Notice:**

## 3. Privacy and Safety Summary

Present these five core commitments directly to the school manager:

1. **Strict Role-Based Visibility:** Parents can only see the bus their child is currently assigned to. Parents of Bus 1 cannot view Bus 2. No parent can see other children's names or home addresses.
2. **Active Trips Only:** Bus location is transmitted and visible *only* while a driver has an active, in-progress trip. When the trip ends, tracking turns off immediately.
3. **Daily Manager Purge & 3-Day Automatic Safety Net:** Managers can purge yesterday's location coordinates with a single click. In addition, an automated system job permanently wipes detailed street coordinates older than 72 hours.
4. **90-Day Summary Reports (Zero Coordinates):** For monthly administrative reporting, the system keeps general statistics (e.g., trip duration, start time, end time, average GPS signal quality) for 90 days. Raw latitude and longitude coordinates are never preserved in summaries.
5. **Tamper-Proof Audit Logging:** Every settings change, student addition, and data purge is permanently stamped in the school's audit log.
6. **Demo Data Assurance:** All names, phone numbers, and addresses shown in this presentation are 100% fictional test data.

## 5. Likely Manager Questions & Plain-Language Answers

### Q1: Is student privacy protected under local laws?
**Answer:** Yes. We practice strict data minimization. The app only requires a child's name, grade, and bus assignment. Parents cannot view other families' information. Detailed GPS coordinates are deleted daily and automatically purged after 3 days.

### Q2: Do parents need to sign a consent form?
**Answer:** Yes. As part of our pilot setup, the school provides a simple one-page parent consent form explaining that the service provides bus location sharing during active school runs.

### Q3: What happens if the driver's phone dies or loses internet connection?
**Answer:** The system handles connection drops gracefully. The parent's screen immediately switches from "LIVE" to "Last seen [X] minutes ago" with an amber warning banner. Parents are never shown fake or stale movement. If the driver's phone turns off, the manager can mark the trip completed from the office dashboard.

### Q4: How accurate is the GPS location?
**Answer:** On modern smartphones with standard mobile data, GPS accuracy is typically within 10 to 15 meters. The driver screen warns the driver if GPS accuracy falls outside 50 meters before they depart.

### Q5: Who owns the transportation data?
**Answer:** Your school owns 100% of its data. We never sell, share, or monetize student or school information. You can request a complete data export or deletion at any time.

### Q6: What happens if the app shows the wrong location?
**Answer:** The parent screen displays the school office telephone number right next to the status card. If parents ever have a question, one tap calls your front desk directly.

### Q7: How do we add parents and assign them to buses?
**Answer:** The school manager can add students and guardian phone numbers one-by-one through the manager portal, or import an Excel/CSV roster during onboarding.

### Q8: What if some of our parents only speak Amharic?
**Answer:** The app has a one-tap language switch right on the login and home screens. Parents can switch back and forth between English and Amharic at any time.

### Q9: What does the system cost?
**Answer:** Our upcoming pilot program is completely free for your school. We want to test on one route, prove the time savings for your office, and gather direct parent feedback before discussing any long-term commercial subscription.

## 6. Pilot Proposal

We propose a low-risk, hands-on pilot to demonstrate measurable value to your school:

```
PILOT AT A GLANCE
• Scope:        1 Bus, 1 Route
• Participants: 15 to 25 Families
• Duration:     3 to 4 Weeks
• Cost:         Free of Charge
```

### Measurable Success Criteria
1. **Phone Call Reduction:** A measurable drop (target: 60%+ reduction) in morning and afternoon phone inquiries to the front desk asking *"Where is the bus?"*
2. **Parent Satisfaction:** Positive survey feedback from at least 80% of participating pilot families regarding pickup predictability.
3. **Driver Usability:** Confirm that starting, delay reporting, and ending trips takes less than 30 seconds of total driver effort per day.
4. **Schedule On-Time Rate:** Improved punctuality at stops due to fewer delays from waiting for unprepared students.

### What the School Must Provide
* **Written Permission:** Formal approval from the school director or transport director to run the pilot.
* **Parent Consent:** Signed consent forms from the 15–25 participating families on the chosen route.
* **Driver Participation & Device:** Agreement from the selected bus driver, and an Android smartphone with an active data package (or school-provided SIM).
* **One Named Contact:** One transport coordinator or office staff member to act as the primary liaison during the 3-week pilot.
